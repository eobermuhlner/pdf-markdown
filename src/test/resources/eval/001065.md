# Employee Conduct and Performance Policy

## Purpose  
This policy establishes expectations for conduct and performance, ensuring a respectful workplace that aligns with **Company**’s values.

---

### Scope  
Applies to all full‑time, part‑time, contract, and temporary staff across every location.

#### 1.1. Definitions  
- **Conduct**: Behaviors affecting teamwork, safety, or reputation.  
- **Performance**: Measurable outcomes against agreed objectives.

## Core Principles  

> “The best way to predict the future is to create it.”  
> — Peter Drucker, 1994

### 2.1. Professionalism  
* Employees must exhibit punctuality, courtesy, and integrity at all times.  
* Sub‑points:  
    - **Dress code**: Business casual unless otherwise specified.  
    - **Communication**: Use respectful language in emails and meetings.

#### 2.2. Performance Management  

> > **Performance reviews** are conducted semi‑annually.  
> — Human Resources, 2023

- Goals set collaboratively with managers.
- Feedback is constructive and documented.

### 3.1. Reporting Violations  
```text
Step 1: Inform your supervisor  
Step 2: File a formal complaint through the HR portal  
Step 3: Follow up within 5 business days
```

> > **Note**: All reports are confidential.  
> — HR Guidelines, 2024

## Accountability Measures  

> **[!NOTE]**  
> Employees found violating policy may face disciplinary action up to termination.

- **Verbal Warning** – first offense.  
- **Written Warning** – second offense.  
- **Suspension or Termination** – third offense or severe violations.

### 4.1. Appeals Process  

| Stage | Action | Timeframe |
|-------|--------|-----------|
| Appeal submitted | HR reviews evidence | Within 7 days |
| Decision | Written notice | Within 5 days |

> **[!WARNING]**  
> Failure to adhere to the appeals process may forfeit further review.

## Continuous Improvement  

> **[!TIP]**  
> Encourage feedback through quarterly surveys and open forums.
