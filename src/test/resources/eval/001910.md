# Employee Conduct and Ethics Policy

**Purpose**  
This policy establishes the standards for professional conduct expected of all employees, ensuring a respectful, inclusive, and compliant workplace.

| **Area** | **Requirement** | **Reporting Mechanism** |
|---|---|---|
| *Conflicts of Interest* | Employees must disclose any personal interests that may influence decisions. | Submit disclosure form via HR portal; review by Ethics Committee. |
> > “Integrity is the cornerstone of our organization.” — *Smith, 2022*  
> > — *Johnson, 2021*  
> > — *Lee & Patel, 2023*  
| **Harassment** | Zero tolerance for any form of harassment or discrimination. | File complaint with HR; investigation within 30 days. |
| ***Compliance*** | Adherence to all applicable laws and regulations. | Mandatory training; annual compliance audit. |

The policy applies to employees at all levels, contractors, and consultants. Violations may result in disciplinary action up to termination, following the **Employee Handbook** guidelines (Doe, 2020).

Employees are encouraged to seek clarification from HR or their supervisor if unsure about any conduct expectations.
