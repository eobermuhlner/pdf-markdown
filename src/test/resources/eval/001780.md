# Rapid Microbial Response to Climate Stressors

## Background  
Recent observations indicate that soil bacteria adapt within hours to temperature spikes, challenging existing ecological models.

### Methods  
- Sampling across five biomes.  
- Genomic sequencing and metabolite profiling.  
- Statistical modeling with Bayesian inference.
