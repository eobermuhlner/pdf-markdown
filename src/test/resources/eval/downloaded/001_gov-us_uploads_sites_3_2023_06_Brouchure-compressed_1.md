# Twenty-Second Judicial Circuit

## Speakers Bureau

### Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I arrange for a speaker for our event?**

Contact Court Administration at 815-334-4385 and ask to speak with the Court Administrator.

**What is the cost for the speaker to attend our event?**

There is no cost associated with speakers from the Twenty-Second Judicial Circuit. It is our pleasure to take the time to educate the community about the court system in McHenry County.

**How much notice is required in order to arrange for a speaker?**

To ensure that we have a speaker, we are requesting at least a 60 day notice; but may accommodate shorter notice.

## Speakers

The Twenty-Second Judicial Circuit of the State of Illinois (McHenry County) provides speakers to present to your community group at no cost. Speakers may include: judges, court administrator or other court officials able to discuss various aspects of the law and the court.

Speakers may be able to travel to your location and provide a general presentation on a legal aspect of the court. In addition, speakers will answer questions of a general nature, however; speakers are not permitted to talk about pending cases, or provide legal advice or advisory opinions.

**TOPICS OF DISCUSSION INCLUDE; BUT ARE NOT LIMITED TO:**

Constitution of the United States of America

Constitution of the State of Illinois

The Three Branches of Government

The Federal Court System

The State Court System

The Twenty-Second Judicial Circuit

The McHenry County Government Center

Court Administration

Drug Court / Mental Health Court Programs

## Reservations

The court needs as much notice as possible, preferably 60 days prior to the presentation date.

**To make a reservation:**

Please download the Request Form on our website, or contact the Court Administration Office at (815) 334-4385 during the hours of 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday through Friday.

You may also contact us by e-mail at courtadmin@22ndcircuit.illinoiscourts.gov

Please include all information relative to your event, organization name and function, date, time, location and number of people attending. Also include the name and telephone number and/or e-mail address of the person to contact.

**Mission Statement**

The mission of the 22nd Judicial Circuit of McHenry County is to be the guardian of life, liberty and property to all seeking access to justice, by adhering to practices that ensure equality, fairness and confidence in the judiciary.
