# Q3 2025 Financial Report

**Executive Summary**  
The company posted a *strong* earnings season, with revenue up 12% YoY and operating margin expanding to 18.5%[^1]. Cash flow from operations increased by $45 M, while capital expenditures rose to $22 M for the acquisition of two regional data centers[^2].

## Key Performance Indicators

| Metric | Q3 2024 | Q3 2025 | YoY % Change |
|--------|---------|---------|--------------|
| Revenue (USD M) | 310.4 | 347.8 | +12.0% |
| Net Income (USD M) | 45.2 | 52.6 | +16.1% |
| EPS (USD) | 3.14 | 3.68 | +17.9% |
| Operating Margin (%) | 16.7 | 18.5 | +1.8 pp |

## Segment Highlights

- **Enterprise Services**: $210 M revenue, 22% growth, driven by cloud adoption.
- **Consumer Products**: $95 M revenue, 5% decline due to seasonal softness.
- **International Markets**: $42 M revenue, +9% in emerging economies.
